# Higgsfield image models (catalog snapshot)

Source: Higgsfield's official model catalog, read on 2026-10-29 through Higgsfield's own tooling (models_explore). No prices here; see pricing.md. Not every model is listed; if asked about one that is missing, say it isn't in your snapshot.

- Higgsfield Soul 2.0 (Higgsfield): realistic UGC, fashion editorial and character generation. Quality tiers 1.5k and 2k. Supports Soul-ID for personalized characters.
- Soul Cinema (Higgsfield): cinema-grade stills and concept art.
- Cinema Studio Image 2.5 (Higgsfield): cinematic stills, up to 4K.
- Marketing Studio Image (Higgsfield): one-click product image ads for social campaigns.
- DTC Ads (Higgsfield): ad images with brand-kit-aware prompts, avatars, products and curated ad formats. Needs a style chosen first.
- GPT Image 2 (OpenAI): 1k/2k/4k, low/medium/high quality tiers; good for text rendering, editing, typography.
- GPT Image 2.5 (OpenAI): generation and editing with Flare and Sunburst variants; quality up to max; can request transparent backgrounds.
- OpenAI Hazel (OpenAI): powerful editing, best text rendering per its catalog description (logos, diagrams, infographics).
- Nano Banana (Google): realistic images, budget-friendly.
- Nano Banana Pro (Google): top quality, text and diagrams; 1k/2k/4k.
- Nano Banana 2 / 2.1 / 2 Lite (Google): fast, high-quality; 2.1 adds image and video references and thinking levels.
- Seedream 4.5 (ByteDance): 4K output, precise control, transformations.
- Seedream 5.0 Lite / Flash / Pro (ByteDance): visual reasoning and instruction-based editing, up to 2K.
- FLUX.2 (Black Forest Labs): pro, flex and max variants, precise prompt adherence; 1k and 2k.
- FLUX 3 Image (Black Forest Labs): generation and editing with up to ten reference images; resolutions up to 4k; batch up to 4.
- Flux Kontext (Black Forest Labs): context-aware editing and style transfer.
- Kling O1 Image (Kling): versatile photorealistic generation.
- Grok Image and Grok Image 2.0 (xAI): expressive, high-contrast generation and editing.
- Ideogram 4.5 (Ideogram): text-to-image and transformation with up to five source images; strong typography.
- Ideogram Vector and Recraft V4.1 (Recraft): SVG and vector output, logos, icons, product mockups.
- Z Image (Tongyi-MAI): super fast, stylized text-to-image.
- AutoSprite Animation (Higgsfield): turns a character image into a game-ready sprite sheet.
- Tools: Topaz upscaling, Bytedance Image Upscale, Image Background Remover, Outpaint, FLUX.2 Pro Outpaint, Image Decompose (splits an image into layers).
