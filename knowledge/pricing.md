# Higgsfield plans and credits

Source: https://higgsfield.ai/blog/higgsfield-pricing-explained
The page says "Prices verified October 2026"; its metadata lists 2026-10-24 as the date modified.
Prices and credit costs change. Always tell the caller these figures are from that page and to check the live pricing page: https://higgsfield.ai/pricing

## Plans
- Free: no credits. Limited use of Nano Banana Pro and FLUX.2 Pro for images, plus one Character generation. Results cannot be used commercially.
- Starter: $15/month, 200 credits, commercial use.
- Plus: $49/month, 1,000 credits.
- Ultra: $129/month, 3,000 credits.
- Team: $79 per seat/month, 1,000 credits per seat, 2 to 9 members.
- Scale: $215 per seat/month, 2,500 credits per seat, 5 to 15 members. Adds Basic SSO and admin spend control.
- Enterprise: for larger teams or custom terms. No price is published; point the caller to Higgsfield.
- Individual plans can be billed monthly or annually. The page does not give annual prices in the table I have; say so and point to the pricing page.

## How credit cost works
"Every generation costs credits, and the cost depends on the model, the clip length, and the resolution."

Examples from that page's table:
- FLUX.2 Pro, 1 image: 1 credit
- Nano Banana Pro, 1 image: 2 credits (4K: 4)
- Seedance 2.0, 5 seconds: 30 credits at 720p, 60 at 1080p, 130 at 4K
- Seedance 2.5, 5 seconds: 35 credits at 720p, 80 at 1080p; 30 seconds at 720p: 210
- Veo 3 Fast with audio, 8 seconds 720p: about 22 credits
- Veo 3 with audio, 8 seconds 720p: about 58 credits

Any model or setting NOT listed above has no cost in this knowledge base. Do not guess it. Say you don't have that number and send the caller to the pricing page or the credits guide.

## Rules
- Subscription credits are spent first, starting with those expiring soonest.
- Failed generations are usually refunded: "If a generation fails, the credits are usually returned automatically."
- Completed generations are not refunded: "If a generation finishes, the credits aren't returned, even if you don't like the result."
- Credits refresh on the billing date (monthly) or every 30 days (annual). Unused credits do not roll over.
- The page estimates Plus credits at about $0.049 each. That is the page's estimate, not a guaranteed rate.

## Unlimited
"Unlimited means some models don't charge credits while the Unlimited toggle is on." There are two kinds:
- 365-day Unlimited Models: Plus and Ultra get the full set. Starter gets only Seedream 5.0 Lite and FLUX.2 Pro (1K). Business plans don't include this set.
- 7-day Unlimited: a shorter window on selected models. When it ends, those models charge credits again. Examples on the page: Nano Banana 2 on Plus monthly, and Kling 3.0 (720p, 5s) on annual plans.
Which exact models are in the unlimited set can change; send the caller to the pricing page for the current list.

## Third-party prices
Blogs and review sites often quote different plan names, prices and credits. Do not use them. Only the Higgsfield page above is a source for this agent.
