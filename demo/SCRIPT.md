## The live-call segment (aim for 60 seconds, trim in the edit)

Open https://hana-comfy-hack.vercel.app, press **Call Higgsy**, allow the mic. Ask these, in this order. If time is
short, cut from the top: question 2 and 3 are the strongest, 4 shows ComfyUI working live.

1. **"What does the Plus plan cost?"** She should say forty-nine dollars a month and one thousand credits, name Higgsfield's pricing page, and warn that prices can change. The pricing links appear on screen.
2. **"How do I cancel my subscription?"** Watch her say "hmm, let me check" and do the little thinking hop, then read the steps from Higgsfield's help article (avatar, Manage Account, Subscription, Plan Settings, cancel).
3. **"How much is the Mega Ultra plan?"** There is no such plan. She should say she can't find it and won't guess. This is the shot that sells "never invents".
4. **"Can you show me an example? A small red paper boat on calm water."** She should confirm, then a picture appears in the side panel with "Made with this ComfyUI workflow" under it. (It takes about 10 to 25 seconds; keep the wait in, or speed it up in the edit.)

She stays still while she or you speak, and only hops while she is quietly thinking.

**Rehearse once first.** I tested the pieces separately (the lookup answers, the image generation, the trap questions in a
simulator) but I haven't heard a live call with a real microphone. If an answer is off, redo the take; don't edit the words.

## Recording tips
- Use Chrome, window about 1280 x 720 or full screen at 1080p, and close other tabs and notifications.
- Capturing her voice: Loom, OBS or CleanShot can record tab or system audio. QuickTime alone can't, so if you use it, play her through speakers and let the mic pick her up, or just rely on the on-screen captions the page already shows.
- The ElevenLabs free tier has a small monthly allowance, so do one rehearsal and one or two takes, not ten.
